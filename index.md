# Vault Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 2026-05-04
**Contact:** garetlx@icloud.com

Vault does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app activity stays on your device.

## What we do NOT do

- No user accounts, no registration, no sign-in
- No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting to third-party services
- No advertising, no marketing identifiers, no fingerprinting
- No third-party SDKs (Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook, etc.)
- No data sent to servers operated by us — Vault has no backend

## What stays on your device

- Downloaded video files in the app's Documents folder (accessible via the iOS Files app)
- App preferences: theme, language, cellular data toggle
- Download history and metadata (titles, posters, playback positions) stored as local JSON files

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it via the iOS Share sheet, AirDrop, or by copying files out through the Files app.

## Permissions

Vault does not request access to:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Photo Library
- Contacts
- Location
- Calendar / Reminders
- HealthKit / Bluetooth

Vault uses standard iOS file picker to import videos from the Files app — this does not require a permission prompt.

## Network usage

Vault makes outbound HTTPS requests only to URLs you explicitly provide:

- Direct video links you paste into the Downloads tab
- URLs you load in the optional built-in browser (developer feature, hidden by default)
- Video and poster URLs detected on pages you visit

All network traffic uses Apple's standard URLSession with App Transport Security enforced (HTTPS, TLS 1.2+).

## Children's privacy

Vault does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Effective" date above will be updated and the new version will be available at the same URL.
